Projects

Development of a Testing System for Quality Inspection of Assembled EV Battery Packs

Another important component in electric vehicle production is the battery pack, which serves as the main power source of the vehicle. Due to its critical role, a dedicated inspection system is required to ensure production consistency and quality assurance. The developed device is designed to assist the quality assurance process after battery cells are assembled into the battery pack. The system verifies the condition of each battery cell by communicating with the battery management MCU through Controller Area Network (CAN) communication.

Smart Control and Monitoring System for Street Lighting using Power Line Carrier Communication (PLCC)

This project focused on monitoring system and controller for public street lighting. There are two different compartment of this project, first is PCB control that installed on the street lighting to monitor and control the lighting. Second compartment is KWH meter that collect data from PCB control through power line carrier communication and send the data to cloud for remote monitoring. The PCB control itself consist of two separated board which is PLCC MCU board and supply board.

Dynamometer for Running and Braking Tests to Enhance Final Inspection in Electric Motorcycle Production

Final inspection of electric vehicle is most important stage of electric vehicle production. There are two section of this device, first section is for running test that allow to measure velocity and maximum speed of electric vehicle. Second section is braking test that measure maximum torque of electric vehicle while barking in specific velocity condition. The device integrated with PC station that allow for data acquisition. UART or serial communication used to connect between microcontroller and PC station, inspector can view every data served through UI or dashboard built using Node-RED.

Development of an EV Relay and Fuse Box Testing System for Quality Inspection

A relay & fuse box tester is a specialized testing device designed as part of the quality control (QC) process on the production line for electric vehicle fuse boxes. Reliable of fuse box is critical as part for main distribution and protection for electricity of electric vehicle. The device designed based on STM32 to automate electrical continuity and functional validation during QC production, improving testing consistency and reducing manual inspection error.

Design and Development of an IoT-Based Dimmable LED Driver for Energy-Efficient Lighting

This project focuses on the development of a 20W LED driver system. The device consists of two different LED types: a 6500K cool white LED and a 3500K warm white LED. The lighting system can be controlled through the Home Assistant application, allowing users to perform brightness dimming and scheduling functions.
